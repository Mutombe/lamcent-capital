Pioneer Transport Secures Investor

Source: The Herald17 August 2017

Pioneer Transport, a former subsidiary of Unifreight, had been under judicial management since its 2015 takeover by employees left it unable to pay all of its creditors. In August 2017, Lamcent Capital was announced as its new investor.

The investment package

  • Ten trucks sold to the company on 12-month credit
  • Fourteen trucks contributed as equity
  • Tyres and working capital to put the fleet back on the road
The judicial manager confirmed the business could keep operating, and drivers were rehired.

On 21 August 2017, Lamcent completed a US$7.5 million equity and debt investment. Creditors were paid off, and Pioneer has since grown into a fleet of more than 150 trucks, buses and specialised units offering cross-border services.

For us, Pioneer shows our approach at work: a defensible business with a strong brand and real demand, held back by its balance sheet rather than its market. With the right capital structure and a focused operating plan, the value already in the business could start earning again.

Perspective

One Project, One Fund, One Board

Lamcent Capital5 minute read

Every project we take on is set up as a separate fund with its own board. It's one of the most important choices we make, and it shapes everything that follows.

Clear accountability

A dedicated board focuses entirely on one asset: its strategy, its risks and its performance. Co-investors see exactly what they own, and management answers to people who know the business in detail.

Operators who share the outcome

We choose technical partners for their experience and proven efficiency. They sign long-term management agreements, and their reward depends on how the asset performs. When the asset does well, so do they.

Room to bring in the right capital

Because each project stands alone, we can raise the mix of equity and debt that suits it, and bring in partners (as we did with SmartVest Wealth Managers on North West High School) without complicating the rest of the portfolio.

Sector Focus · Education

Closing the Private-Education Gap

Lamcent Capital4 minute read

Zimbabwe has a real gap in high-quality private education. Families want strong academic results, good facilities and safe environments, at fees they can sustain. Our answer is the Lamcent Educational Trust.

  • Crystal Trust Primary School in Glaudina
  • North West High School, a boarding school in Madokero, Westgate, estimated at US$8 million and partly funded with SmartVest Wealth Managers
  • Enterprise Primary School in Gletwyn, Shawasha, now under construction

Location is everything. We look for prime sites where affordable, high-quality schooling can serve growing communities for decades. The result is social infrastructure the country needs, and a long-term asset that holds its value.

Strategy

Preserving Value in a Volatile Economy

Lamcent Capital4 minute read

Our core idea is simple: in an economy where currency and inflation can wipe out returns, the most important thing a private equity firm can offer is value preservation. That's why we focus on alternative investments in asset classes that earn foreign currency and resist inflation.

What that looks like in practice

  • Export agriculture: beef, citrus and lucerne for European and Asian markets
  • Industrial minerals such as limestone, sold to power, agriculture, cement and paint
  • Hard assets like hotels, schools and property that keep their real value
  • Cross-border logistics that bill regional customers

We aim for growth above inflation on our investments while managing the risks that come with it. We do that through governance, carefully chosen partners and patience.
