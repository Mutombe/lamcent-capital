Case Study · Pioneer Transport

From judicial management to a 150+ fleet

2015

Employee takeover

The former Unifreight subsidiary is taken over by its employees but can't pay all its creditors and enters judicial management.

17 Aug 2017

Lamcent announced as investor

Ten trucks on 12-month credit, 14 trucks as equity, plus tyres and working capital. The judicial manager confirms the business can keep operating.

21 Aug 2017

US$7.5M equity and debt closes

Creditors are paid off and drivers are rehired as the fleet returns to the road.

Today

Cross-border operator

A fleet of more than 150 trucks, buses and specialised units serving regional routes.
