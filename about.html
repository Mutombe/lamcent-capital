Who We Are

Alternative Investments
Built for Value Preservation

Lamcent Capital (Private) Limited is a private equity firm in Zimbabwe. We offer alternative investments designed to preserve value, focused on asset classes that earn foreign currency and resist inflation.

We provide capital to small and medium-sized businesses across the country, on both brownfield and greenfield projects, and we select every opportunity on its return, its contribution to economic growth, its ability to preserve value and, where it fits, the chance to partner with Government on social services.

Our Roots

Finance Began
at Home

Before there were funds, there were households: families who kept value across generations, extended credit to people they trusted and judged a venture by the character of the person behind it.

The tools have changed. Boards replaced parlours and term sheets replaced handshakes. The principles haven't. Lamcent Capital is built on that older idea of stewardship: preserve what you hold, grow it patiently and pass on more than you received.

ThenFamily stewardshipValue kept within trusted circles, measured in generations.
NowInstitutional disciplineSeparate funds, independent boards and performance-linked partners.
